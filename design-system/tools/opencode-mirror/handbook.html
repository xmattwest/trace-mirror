Your working style, conversation fingerprint and daily rhythm, drawn from your local archive.

The report

  1. Working stylePersona and communication fingerprint

    A reading of how you ask questions, direct work and review results. Scoring rules and message counts are available in the report.

  2. Daily rhythmWeekday × hour

    A mosaic of when your conversations gather, in your local timezone. Exact counts are one click away.

  3. A snapshot to keepPNG card or offline HTML

    Save your style and rhythm without prompts, tool inputs or source paths. The HTML export works offline.

Run locally

python3 mirror.py --db "/path/to/opencode.db"

Unzip the download and run the command from that folder. Mirror opens http://127.0.0.1:8787/. Without --db, it tries the usual OpenCode data directory or lets you enter a path.

Timezone and headless export
python3 mirror.py --db "/path/to/opencode.db" --timezone America/Denver
python3 mirror.py --db "/path/to/opencode.db" --export mirror.html

The app uses your browser’s timezone; headless exports use the computer’s zone, with UTC as a fallback. An explicit override takes precedence. Daylight saving follows each message’s date, and exports retain their original zone.

Method and privacy

The persona is a deterministic heuristic, not a validated personality test. Fewer than 20 filtered messages produces an early sketch. Root user text excludes synthetic/ignored parts, reminders and identifiable history copies. Pasted material can still influence the result. Activity measures interaction, not hours worked.

The database is opened read-only. Nothing is uploaded. Exports exclude prompts, tool inputs and outputs, full paths, session IDs and credentials. Shared HTML aliases project names; dates, aggregate counts and model/tool names remain.
