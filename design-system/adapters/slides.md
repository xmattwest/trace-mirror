# Slides and presentations

## Purpose and pacing

One argumentative beat per slide. A five-slide explanation can follow: question → design → result → qualification → implication. Use assertion titles instead of category labels. Notes can carry depth, but qualifications required to interpret the result stay visible.

## Geometry and typography

Default authoring canvas: 1600 × 900 (16:9), with 96 px safe margins. Profile body is 32 px, supporting text 24 px, headings 44 px, display 68 px, lede 38 px. Prestige on slides comes from restraint: few short statements, one dominant visual, and titles that fit without shrinking. This replaces document-sized text on projected slides. Judge the final viewing distance; display values are starting points.

Use few short statements and one dominant visual. Charts need slide-specific label sizes and geometry. Rebuild a figure for a slide rather than enlarging a small screenshot. Use flat Ivory background, dark type, and one accent event. Native slide-export units may differ; preserve proportions and final legibility.

## Delivery and review

The example deck is a static, vertically browsable HTML deck with five named sections, keyboard-accessible navigation, and landscape print pages. Its PDF is a portable presentation export; the HTML and builder are its editable source.

Check all five frames at presentation size, sources and sample sizes on results slides, slide order, no clipped content, and exactly five landscape pages. For a narrow-screen companion view, stack content with readable text; that companion is not the projected canvas.
